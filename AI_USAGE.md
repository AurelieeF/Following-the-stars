# Use of AI — You Will Find Me in the Stars (team AuFX, Lily)

NASA Space Apps asks every team to say clearly where and how AI was used. Here is mine.

## What I did
- **The idea, the story and the characters.** The boy, his robot, his mom the astronaut, the promise, the letter, the ending. All mine.
- **The game design.** The two missions, what the player has to choose, the NASA photos as the reward, the arcade.
- **The base code.** I started the game myself in JavaScript.
- **The 3D.** I started the astronaut in Blender, then moved the 3D into the browser with Three.js.
- **The crying sound on the Moon.** It's my own voice; I recorded it.
- **Testing.** I played every version, decided what stays and what changes.

## How AI (Claude) helped me
- Cleaning up and organizing my code, adding comments, and fixing small problems and bugs.
- Helping me with the Three.js rendering and smoothing the animations.
- Building some of the features I asked for after playtesting (the mission report and score, team mode, the power and data limits, the arcade).
- Helping me write the French and English dialogue from my story. I reviewed every line.

## Not AI
- The NASA photos are real NASA data (EPIC/DSCOVR, NASA Image and Video Library).
- The music and the other sound effects are generated in code (Web Audio API).

AI use is also mentioned in the game's end credits.
